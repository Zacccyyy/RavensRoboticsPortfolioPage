An idea in progress: a small e-ink panel, framed like a piece of art, that
passively listens to a room's ambient audio, fingerprints whatever song is
playing, and displays its lyrics timed to the music. No companion app, no
account, no phone in hand — it just knows what's playing and shows it.

## Current state

Still at the concept stage. The open questions are audio fingerprinting
latency on a Pi 5 and whether lyric-timing data is reliably available for
fingerprint matches without a paid API.
