A standard feeder retrofitted with a low-power camera and an on-device
classifier that identifies bird species as they land, logging each visit
with a timestamp, species, and confidence score to a local database — no
cloud service, no subscription, no footage leaving the device.

## Current state

Shipped and running year-round. Species coverage is tuned to the birds
common to the builder's region; retraining on a different region's species
list is a documented, if manual, process.
